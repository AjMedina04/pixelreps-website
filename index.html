PIXELREPS

An 8-bit workout logger for iPhone. Your hero levels up when you do.

WHAT IT IS

PixelReps turns your gym log into a retro RPG. Every workout is a quest, every set earns XP, and your pixel hero evolves as you get stronger. Daily and weekly quests, streaks, trophies, and PR fanfares keep you coming back. No subscription, no account, no ads.
