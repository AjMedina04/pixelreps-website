PRIVACY POLICY

Effective June 11, 2026

PixelReps is built so that your data never leaves your device. This policy explains exactly what that means.

DATA WE COLLECT

None. PixelReps does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to the internet at all.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES

Your workouts, hero profile, settings, and progress are stored locally on your iPhone in the app's private storage. They are included in your normal device backups (iCloud or computer backups) under the same protections Apple applies to all app data. We have no access to any of it.

EXPORTS

You can export your workout history as CSV or JSON files from the settings screen. Exports only happen when you start them, and the resulting files go only where you choose to send them through the iOS share sheet.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you enable rest timer alerts, PixelReps schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered entirely on your iPhone and are never sent to a server.

CHILDREN

PixelReps does not collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the App Store release notes.
