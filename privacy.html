PRIVACY POLICY

Effective July 30, 2026

PixelReps collects nothing about you. Your training log lives on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. It never reaches us. This policy explains exactly what that means.

DATA WE COLLECT

None. PixelReps does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We operate no servers and receive nothing about you or your training.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES

Your workouts, hero profile, settings, and progress are stored on your iPhone in the app's private storage, and are included in your normal device backups under the same protections Apple applies to all app data.

If you are signed in to iCloud, that same data is also mirrored to your own private iCloud database, so it survives deleting the app or moving to a new phone. That copy belongs to your Apple account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer of PixelReps cannot read it, query it, or recover it for you. Turning off iCloud for PixelReps in iOS Settings stops the mirroring; deleting the app removes the local copy.

PURCHASES

PixelReps offers one optional purchase, the Founder's Pack, which grants cosmetic items and in-game coins. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase completed.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you enable rest timer alerts, PixelReps schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated and delivered entirely on your iPhone and are never sent to a server.

CHILDREN

PixelReps does not collect data from anyone, including children.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the App Store release notes.
